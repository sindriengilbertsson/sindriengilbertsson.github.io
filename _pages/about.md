I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Amsterdam, specialising in industrial organisation and competition policy. My research examines cartel enforcement, sustainability cooperation and market entry. My interest in competition policy began while working at the Icelandic Competition Authority. I enjoyed applying economic analysis to practical questions that affect people's everyday lives, and that experience motivated me to pursue a PhD in the field.

In 2025, I spent a year at the OECD's Directorate for Employment, Labour and Social Affairs (ELS), working on post-employment restrictions in labour contracts, including non-compete clauses. I continue to work on these questions with colleagues at the OECD.
